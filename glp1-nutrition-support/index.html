This is nutrition support—not medication management.

I do not prescribe, adjust or advise you to stop medication. Questions about medication belong with your prescribing healthcare provider.

Where nutrition fits

Less appetite can make enough nutrition harder.

When appetite or meal size changes, it may take more intention to eat in a way that remains nourishing. Nutritional counselling gives you space to look at what you are eating, what feels manageable and where a few clear adjustments could help.

The goal is not to hand you another perfect plan. It is to find practical ways to support adequate nutrition within your appetite, preferences, routine and the guidance of your healthcare team.

What we can work on

Protein

Identify realistic protein foods and ways to spread them through the day.

Balanced eating

Build smaller meals and snacks that still contribute useful nutrition.

Eating patterns

Create a workable rhythm when hunger cues or appetite feel different.

Practical choices

Use food options that fit your tolerance, preferences, schedule and daily life.

If symptoms, side effects or intake concerns need medical assessment, I will encourage you to speak with your prescribing provider or another appropriate regulated professional.
