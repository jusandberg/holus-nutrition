Hōlus Digest

Know your nutrients.

What each one does, where to find it, and what can happen when you’re low.

Macro

Protein

Builds and repairs your body, and helps meals keep you full.

What it does

  • Builds and repairs muscle, skin and organs
  • Makes enzymes, hormones and antibodies
  • Helps keep you full between meals
  • Needs often rise with age and training

If you’re low

  • Loss of muscle and strength
  • Slower healing and recovery
  • Feeling hungry soon after eating
  • Lower resistance to infection

Where to find it

  • Eggs
  • Fish
  • Greek yogurt
  • Lentils & beans
  • Nuts & seeds
Macro

Carbohydrates

Your body’s preferred fuel, for your brain and your muscles.

What it does

  • Main energy source for brain and muscles
  • Fuels training and recovery
  • Whole-grain types bring fibre, vitamins and minerals

If you’re low

  • Tiredness and low energy
  • Trouble concentrating
  • Weaker workouts and slower recovery

Where to find it

  • Whole grains
  • Rice & oats
  • Fruit
  • Starchy veg
  • Legumes
Macro

Fats

Needed to absorb key vitamins and to support your brain and hormones.

What it does

  • Helps absorb vitamins A, D, E and K
  • Builds cell walls and supports the brain
  • Needed to make some hormones
  • Omega-3s support heart health

If you’re low

  • Dry, flaky skin
  • Poor absorption of vitamins A, D, E and K
  • Meals that do not keep you satisfied

Where to find it

  • Olive oil
  • Avocado
  • Nuts
  • Fatty fish
  • Seeds
Macro

Fibre

Keeps digestion regular and feeds the good bacteria in your gut.

What it does

  • Keeps digestion regular
  • Feeds healthy gut bacteria
  • Helps steady blood sugar after meals
  • Helps lower LDL cholesterol

If you’re low

  • Constipation
  • Bigger blood-sugar swings
  • Feeling less full after meals

Where to find it

  • Oats
  • Beans & lentils
  • Vegetables
  • Fruit
  • Whole grains
Micro

Iron

Carries oxygen in your blood to every part of your body.

What it does

  • Carries oxygen in red blood cells
  • Supports energy and focus
  • Vitamin C helps your body absorb iron from plants

If you’re low

  • Tiredness and weakness
  • Pale skin and feeling cold
  • Shortness of breath
  • More common with heavy periods or plant-based eating

Where to find it

  • Red meat & poultry
  • Lentils & beans
  • Leafy greens
  • Pumpkin seeds
  • Fortified cereals
Micro

Calcium

Keeps bones and teeth strong, and helps muscles and nerves work.

What it does

  • Builds and maintains bones and teeth
  • Helps muscles contract
  • Supports nerve signals and heart rhythm

If you’re low

  • Lower bone density over time
  • Higher risk of fractures and osteoporosis
  • Needs matter more in midlife and after menopause

Where to find it

  • Milk & fortified plant milk
  • Yogurt
  • Cheese
  • Canned salmon with bones
  • Almonds
Micro

Vitamin D

Helps your body use calcium, and supports muscles and immunity.

What it does

  • Helps absorb calcium for strong bones
  • Supports muscle function
  • Plays a role in immune health

If you’re low

  • Weaker bones
  • Bone or muscle aches
  • Muscle weakness

Where to find it

  • Sunlight, limited in Canadian winters
  • Fatty fish
  • Egg yolks
  • Fortified milk
Micro

Vitamin B12

Keeps your nerves and red blood cells healthy.

What it does

  • Helps make healthy red blood cells
  • Supports nerves and the brain
  • Helps turn food into energy

If you’re low

  • Tiredness and low energy
  • Tingling or numbness in hands and feet
  • Memory or mood changes
  • More common with age, plant-based eating or some medications

Where to find it

  • Fish & meat
  • Eggs
  • Dairy
  • Fortified plant foods
Micro

Magnesium

Involved in hundreds of processes, from muscles to energy.

What it does

  • Helps muscles and nerves work
  • Supports energy production
  • Contributes to bone health

If you’re low

  • Muscle cramps
  • Tiredness
  • Weakness

Where to find it

  • Nuts & seeds
  • Leafy greens
  • Beans & lentils
  • Whole grains

General information, not personal advice. Talk to a qualified professional before making changes to your diet or supplements.
