What it is

Nutrition education you can use.

Nutritional counselling is a collaborative conversation about your nutrition, eating patterns, routines, preferences, goals and priorities. We make sense of what matters, identify a small number of useful areas to focus on and translate them into realistic next steps.

You do not need a perfectly defined goal or a diagnosis before you book. We can begin with what is changing, what feels confusing or what you want to feel more confident about.

It may be a good fit if you want support with

  • building balanced, nourishing meals
  • energy, appetite and everyday eating patterns
  • protein, fibre, hydration and other nutrition fundamentals
  • food choices, meal planning and grocery shopping
  • nutrition through midlife or alongside an active lifestyle
  • making sense of conflicting nutrition information
  • habits and behaviour change that work in real life

Nutritional counselling supports medical care and does not replace it. When something needs medical assessment or care beyond nutrition education, referral is a normal part of good care.

FAQ

Good to know before you book.

What happens in a first session?

We have a comprehensive conversation about your nutrition, lifestyle, routines, goals and priorities. You’ll leave with clear areas of focus, a small number of practical recommendations we’ve discussed together and relevant resources to support you afterward.

Do I need a diagnosis or referral?

No. You can book directly. If you are under the care of a physician or another practitioner, nutrition support can work alongside them.

What are follow-up appointments for?

Follow-ups give us time to review what is working, navigate barriers and adjust the approach together. They create room to learn, practise and refine without expecting everything to change at once.

Are appointments virtual or in person?

Contact Juliana to confirm the appointment formats and locations currently available before booking.

How do I book?

Use the booking links on this site to email Juliana about an initial consultation or a free 15-minute discovery call. A direct booking platform can replace these links when selected.

What is outside your scope?

Diagnosing conditions, treating disease, interpreting medical tests, and prescribing or adjusting medication. Nutritional counselling supports medical care; it does not replace it.

Can you work alongside my physician or another practitioner?

Yes. Nutritional counselling can complement care from your physician, prescribing healthcare provider or another practitioner while staying within nutritional counselling scope.

When would you refer me elsewhere?

When something needs medical assessment, belongs with another regulated professional or calls for care beyond nutrition education. Referral is a normal part of good care.

Are appointments covered by insurance or benefits?

Many extended health plans cover nutritional consulting, but coverage varies. Confirm directly with your provider. A receipt with the RNCP, NNCP and RSSW designations is issued after each session.
